ATS Resume Agent for Pharmacy Students

Made for B.Pharm, M.Pharm, Pharm.D and D.Pharm students. Pick any pharmacy job role you see on LinkedIn (QC, QA, R&D, regulatory, pharmacovigilance, clinical research, hospital, retail, sales and more), add your master resume, and the agent builds a tailored, ATS-friendly one-column resume. It only uses what you wrote and never invents experience or numbers.

How to use (2 minutes)
  1. Fill your master resume once. Include everything, even rough bullets and things that may not fit. Add numbers only where they are true.
  2. Paste the job description and company name for each job you apply to.
  3. Press Run agent. Read the report, tick only the skills you genuinely have, then download.
  4. You can click into the resume preview and edit any text before downloading.

Your data stays in this browser (saved locally). Nothing is uploaded.
